Pricing pages can combine multiple states. SiteGround and Hostinger illustrative figures are from different term lengths and are not matched quotes.

- **Testing:** no hands-on evidence, screenshots or matched hosting benchmarks exist. Research is Mode A and all five hosting pages use the same hypothetical matched WordPress workload; no provider has a tested performance score or winner. Mode B requires reviewed evidence and per-product constraints.
- **Affiliate:** check Program Status, Verification Status, Application Status and Approval Status separately. None of these drafts establishes a new application or approval. Jasper, HubSpot, Asana and other unverified programs require validation; Hostinger is deferred and Ahrefs is discontinued. No Hostinger or Ahrefs affiliate CTA is included. Affiliate terms baseline expires 2026-12-02. Never guess an affiliate ID or treat a manually confirmed fallback as automatically nontracking.
- **Internal sibling rule:** Several registry clusters contain fewer than three commercial pages: small-business hosting has only this one; CRM only HubSpot; AI writing has two; SEO has two; email marketing has two. Cross-cluster links are not siblings. Obtain a real approved expansion or explicit resolution; never invent destination URLs. All links are conditional on published targets.
- **Template gaps:** Stage 1 verdict shortcode may emit a CTA before proof and dynamic price components cannot represent unverified values safely, so written verdicts and draft manual tables are used. Comparison tables are static copy; the shortcode matrix with verified tool records and no premature CTA needs separate staging integration. Do not imply that a template shortcode, visual formatting or fallback link has been tested.
- **Schema and operations:** Graphs are specifications, not tested rendered JSON-LD. Align actual visible breadcrumb hierarchy, author information, one schema provider, SEO, disclosure/CTA, accessibility, cache, contact and legal pages before any publication. No WordPress, frontend, accessibility, performance or live-site tests have occurred.
