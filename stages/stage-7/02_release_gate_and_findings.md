## Prioritized findings and exit criteria

1. **P0-01: No implemented staging/site.** Owner supplies isolated, access-controlled staging and authorizes its use; follow `stages/stage-6/bundle/README.md`. Assemble layouts, integrations and chrome, import drafts only, inspect real IDs, and rerun this gate on actual previews. Never auto-publish or change the live homepage. Verify actual WP-CLI behavior on staging; its wrapper has not been tested.
2. **P0-02: Evidence unavailable.** Complete and retain dated, plan-specific Stage-1 logs/screenshots and editorial approval for each Mode A page; upgrade to Mode B only with actual evidence. Do not infer observations. Evidence gate checks attachment presence, not truth.
3. **P0-03: Missing facts and 128 tokens.** Resolve the individual token inventory from current first-party sources, preserve correct unit, term and region, and update citations. If a value cannot be verified, keep it blocked or omit the claim after editorial review. Recheck freshness at release, not only at audit.
4. **P0-04: Owner/legal unknown.** Verify actual legal operator, authors/reviewers, contact endpoint, correction process, analytics, consent, privacy, hosting and terms; obtain qualified legal review and remove or resolve every owner marker. Contact form and newsletter must not collect until configured and reviewed.
5. **P0-05: Affiliate baseline expiration.** 2026-09-03 program baseline expires **2026-12-02** (13 days after this audit). Reverify each program independently from its account and first-party terms, including the four separate fields and source dates. Fix stale “Formalized” values when application is Not Applied/Unknown. Do not change current statuses by inference or paste credentials. Review `stages/stage-0/04_affiliate_action_sheet.md` before seeding.
6. **P0-06: Schema/canonical/index.** Establish a single graph owner and resolve Rank Math/other plugin overlap, validate HTML and graph, page-specific policy, actual breadcrumb hierarchy and sitemap behavior. Source graph examples with simplified breadcrumbs do not prove the rendered graph.
7. **P0-07: Build and cache gaps.** Stage 3 has 15 scaffolds, not Elementor pages. T12/404 are only specs; archive, comparison, search and 404 integrations are not built. Cluster grid can include itself/unrelated matches and caps results at 12; homepage cannot select Wave 1, directory lacks a bespoke tagline, comparison matrix renders blank with <2 records. Breadcrumbs can expose a draft ancestor. Fix/test or use approved safe manual fallbacks before exposing them; ensure every dynamic widget has Inactive caching.
8. **P0-08: Safe deployment/recovery.** Importer is non-transactional; previous inserts can remain and a failed insert can lack its ownership marker. Back up staging, inspect existing routes (including default privacy draft), get approval, dry-run and reconcile any partial import. Seeding and published synced patterns require separate authorization. Source hashes, production hostname refusal and `blog_public=0` are safeguards, not proof a host is safe.
9. **P1-01: Complete real-device/browser, accessibility, link, tracking and CWV tests.** Record timestamp, environment, URL, outcome and evidence. Investigate the 38/50 keyword-universe shortfall and obtain genuine SEO tool exports; never invent volume, KD or CPC. Keep Wave-3 Finance/Insurance and head terms on HOLD, without planning or creating pages.
