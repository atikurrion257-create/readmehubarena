# Wave-2 trigger checklist — from research to verified hands-on mode

A completed worksheet pasted by the owner is a trigger to **review**, not automatic proof of every claim. Switch only the page/product whose actual dated evidence meets the criteria; a ClickUp test does not establish Systeme.io, Trello, Asana, Notion or a matched comparison. Do not start Stage 5 or publish solely because this checklist has been satisfied.

1. Confirm real tester, date (not future), actual plan and region; describe each workflow, the actual steps, timing scope, 1–5 friction rubric (5 = most), observations, limits, upgrade triggers, deal-breakers, failed/untested steps and screenshot filenames. Redact personal and credential data.
2. Review the full dated log and actual screenshots for consistency and authenticity; attach the files to the specific staging WordPress page. A pasted worksheet without accessible supporting attachments does not satisfy the Stage 1 plugin's Mode B gate. No claim should exceed the observed plan, conditions or evidence.
3. Assign `rmh_evidence_mode=mode_b_tested`, `rmh_plan_tested`, `rmh_test_date`, `rmh_workflow_evaluated`, `rmh_observed_limits`, `rmh_test_log_attachment`, `rmh_test_screenshot_attachment`, and `rmh_evidence_verified=1` only after real editorial review. Record a visible, truthful reviewed product name when eligible for Review schema. The runtime checks presence and file existence, not evidentiary truth.
4. Rewrite the relevant page's status, verdict and evidence language to describe actual observations; remove or qualify untested statements. Re-verify current prices and affiliate statuses separately. Ratings remain blank unless supported by a disclosed rubric and visible evidence; the candidate plugin intentionally emits no rating schema.
5. Roundups/alternatives require matched evidence for each product before claiming a hands-on ranking; otherwise keep their research designation and clearly distinguish the single tested product. Do not imply a product-specific worksheet changes the evaluation mode of untested competitors.
6. Verify disclosure placement, live shortcode behavior, schema, source dates, links, caching, WordPress rendering and editorial/legal approval on staging before any release. Until then remain draft/noindex.
